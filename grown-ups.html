About this app

The Play Room is a set of simple tools to use alongside therapy sessions or at home: a feelings check-in, guided breathing, and weekly action plans.

Privacy

This is a static site. Action plans are saved only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Tips

  • Sit with your child and explore the activities together.
  • This app supports, but does not replace, professional care.

Made with care. Everything stays on this device.
